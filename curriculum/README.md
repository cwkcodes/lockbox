# Energy, Markets, Finance and Wealth Programme

An auditable, sequenced learning programme taking a UK renewable-energy engineer to
commercial and financial competence across energy assets, markets and businesses.

**Participant:** Mechanical & Renewable Energy Engineer, Renewable Energy Consultant at
Locogen, Scotland. ~30 years old.
**Programme start:** August 2026.
**Objective:** capability to understand, model, challenge and eventually allocate capital
across energy assets, markets and businesses.

## Standards this programme holds itself to

Every claim in these files is tagged so you can audit it:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[FACT]** | Verifiable from a cited primary or official source |
| **[CALC]** | Arithmetic derived from stated inputs — you can reproduce it |
| **[ASSUMPTION]** | An input chosen for illustration, not measured |
| **[ESTIMATE]** | A quantified judgement based on partial evidence |
| **[OPINION]** | My judgement, which you should challenge |

Data sources are cited inline with the date accessed. Where data quality is poor —
salary aggregators being the worst offender — this is stated rather than hidden.

Illustrative figures are labelled as such. No market data, salary range or project
assumption in this repository is invented and presented as real.

## What this programme will not do

- Recommend individual shares, cryptocurrencies or speculative trades.
- Promise that £200k+ compensation or a multi-million-pound net worth will occur.
- Pretend that every one of the 22 module groups needs deep expertise. Most need
  working knowledge; several need only awareness. This is stated per module.
- Substitute reading for building. Every module ends in a deliverable.

## Time and realism

Genuine commercial competence in this field is a **three-to-five year** project at
10 hours per week alongside full-time work, not a six-month one. The programme is
built to produce employable evidence much earlier than that — the first portfolio
project is designed to be interview-ready within 90 days — but the honest statement
is that you are converting a specialist engineering identity into a
technical-commercial one, and that conversion is measured in years.

What compresses the timeline is not study hours. It is getting transaction exposure
inside your current job. See `08-networking-and-positioning.md`, section 3.
